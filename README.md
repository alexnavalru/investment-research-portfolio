## Research Approach

My default process is:

1. Understand the business model and industry structure.
2. Rebuild the relevant financial history rather than relying only on headline adjusted metrics.
3. Study management, capital allocation and balance-sheet risk.
4. Define the variant perception or source of mispricing.
5. Build base, upside and downside scenarios.
6. Identify catalysts and thesis-breaking risks.
7. Size positions according to downside, timing and confidence.
8. Review outcomes and update the process when the thesis is wrong.
