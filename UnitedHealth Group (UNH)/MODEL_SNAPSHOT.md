# UnitedHealth Group (UNH) - Valuation Snapshot

This snapshot summarizes the valuation framework used in the 2025 thesis.

## Earnings Reset

| Item | Adjusted EPS |
|---|---:|
| 2024 actual | **$27.66** |
| Initial 2025 guidance | **$29.50–$30.00** |
| April 2025 revised guidance | **$26.00–$26.50** |
| May 2025 | **Guidance suspended** |

The investment case did **not** assume that the original 2025 guidance would be achieved. The thesis was built around a period of depressed profitability followed by normalization as pricing and operating changes caught up with medical-cost trend.

## Original Valuation Framework

The working model used three main cross-checks:

1. **P/E on normalized earnings**
2. **Price / FCF**
3. **EV / EBITDA**

The objective was to avoid anchoring the thesis to one accounting metric during a period of unusually high uncertainty.

## 2027 Valuation Cross-Checks From the Working Model

The current version of the personal model produces approximately:

| Method | 2027 value |
|---|---:|
| P/E cross-check | **~$428/share** |
| P/FCF cross-check | **~$405/share** |
| EV/EBITDA cross-check | **~$430/share** |

These outputs are broadly consistent with the original **$400–$450** medium-term target.


## Why Normalization Was Plausible

The underlying assumption was not that medical inflation would disappear.

It was that the economics of the plans could improve as UnitedHealth:

- repriced risk products;
- redesigned benefits;
- exited uneconomic plans;
- narrowed networks where necessary;
- improved payment integrity and utilization management;
- adjusted commercial pricing as contracts renewed.

By Q2 2025, management explicitly described its 2026 pricing strategy as focused on margin recovery. It said Medicare Advantage pricing would assume a medical-cost trend approaching **10%**, versus the approximately **7.5%** trend then expected for 2025.

## What Could Break the Valuation

A normalized valuation would be wrong if:

- medical costs rose faster than pricing for several years;
- regulatory pressure permanently reduced achievable margins;
- Optum's economics deteriorated structurally;
- membership losses offset pricing improvements;
- legal liabilities materially impaired the balance sheet or franchise.

## Instrument Choice

The thesis used long-dated calls because the timing of normalization was uncertain.

That created a different risk profile from owning the shares:

**correct business thesis + wrong timing = potentially poor option outcome**

For that reason, duration and position sizing were central to the trade construction.
