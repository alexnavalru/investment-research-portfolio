## Look-Through Conservative NAV

I use NAV here only for **separable assets**. I do not include the operating assets required to generate JD Retail earnings and then add an operating multiple on top, because that would double count value.

### Step 1 — Deconsolidated excess cash

After excluding **RMB13.4bn of restricted cash**, Q2 2026 unrestricted liquid assets and financial debt imply approximately **RMB151.8bn of unrestricted net financial cash**.

To avoid double counting the cash already embedded in the quoted market values of listed subsidiaries, I remove the full net cash / net debt of JD Health, JD Industrials and JD Logistics from the consolidated amount.

This leaves approximately **RMB92.6bn** of parent / residual-group unrestricted net cash.

I then reserve **RMB30bn** for working capital, strategic investment and acquisitions.

**Excess cash credited to NAV: ~RMB62.6bn.**

### Step 2 — Listed subsidiaries / spin-offs

Current market values are converted into RMB and multiplied by JD's ownership. A further **20% haircut** is applied.

| Listed subsidiary | JD ownership | Conservative attributable value |
|---|---:|---:|
| JD Health | **67%** | **RMB51.3bn** |
| JD Logistics | **63%** | **RMB28.2bn** |
| JD Industrials | **71%** | **RMB16.9bn** |

These stakes are valued separately, so their operating profits are removed from the residual operating SOTP below where necessary.

### Step 3 — Private / financial assets

| Asset | Conservative treatment | Value |
|---|---:|---:|
| JD Technology | 80% of carrying value | **RMB12.0bn** |
| Home Credit direct stake | 80% of carrying / acquisition value | **RMB1.9bn** |
| Residual equity-method investments | 25% of residual carrying value | **RMB10.1bn** |
| JD Property | Conservative placeholder | **RMB10.0bn** |
| Marketable securities & other investments | 50% of carrying value | **RMB19.2bn** |
| Dada / other New Businesses | No separate positive NAV | **RMB0bn** |
| CECONOMY | No value until closing | **RMB0bn** |

JD Property is deliberately valued far below its **RMB121.5bn AUM**, because assets under management are not shareholder equity value.

## Penalized Residual Operating SOTP

JD Retail H1 2026 operating profit was **RMB28.438bn**.

Because JD Health and JD Industrials are already included at market value in NAV, I strip their H1 operating income from Retail:

**RMB28.438bn − RMB3.174bn − RMB0.530bn = RMB24.734bn H1 residual core EBIT**

Annualized:

**~RMB49.468bn residual JD Retail EBIT**

I value this residual core at only **4.0x EV/EBIT**.

### New Businesses drag

H1 2026 New Businesses operating loss:

**RMB20.206bn**

Annualized current run-rate drag:

**~RMB40.412bn**

The model therefore shows three scenarios.

| Scenario | Core EBIT | New Businesses drag | Net EBIT | 4x Operating SOTP |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| **Current drag persists** | 49.468 | (40.412) | 9.056 | **RMB36.224bn** |
| **50% loss normalization** | 49.468 | (20.206) | 29.262 | **RMB117.048bn** |
| **New Businesses break-even** | 49.468 | 0.000 | 49.468 | **RMB197.872bn** |

The current-drag case is an intentionally extreme stress test. The break-even case gives New Businesses **zero positive value**; it simply stops penalizing the core.

### Why the >$35 target does not require a heroic New Businesses outcome

A $35 ADR corresponds to total equity value of roughly **RMB315bn** on the same share count and FX.

After subtracting the conservative RMB212.3bn NAV, the operating businesses only need to justify about **RMB90bn**.

At 4x EBIT, that requires approximately **RMB22–23bn of net operating EBIT**, meaning New Businesses could still be losing roughly **RMB27bn annually**.

In other words, the current annualized New Businesses loss only needs to decline by roughly **one third** for the model to support a value around $35 per ADR.

This is why **>$35 per ADR** remains my medium-term target rather than using the much higher full break-even value as the base case.

## EV / EBIT Multiple Sensitivity

The headline SOTP uses **4x EV/EBIT deliberately as a conservative anchor**. I do not view 4x as JD Retail's normalized fair multiple. A conservative sensitivity range of **4x–6x** is useful to show how much upside exists if the market applies a less punitive valuation.

| EV / EBIT | Current drag persists | Losses fall 50% | New Businesses break-even |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| **4x** | **~$27.6** | **~$36.5** | **~$45.5** |
| **5x** | **~$28.6** | **~$39.8** | **~$51.0** |
| **6x** | **~$29.6** | **~$43.0** | **~$56.5** |

The purpose of keeping **4x** as the headline case is to preserve a wide margin of safety. Any re-rating toward 5x–6x materially increases the implied ADR value.
