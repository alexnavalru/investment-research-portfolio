# JD.com (JD) — Model Snapshot

This page combines selected outputs from my internal Excel model with the latest reported Q2 2026 data.

## Current Reference

- ADR reference price: **~$26.50**
- Internal model ordinary-share equivalent: **~RMB86.95**
- Model market capitalization: **~RMB250.0bn**
- Model net cash: **~RMB151.8bn**
- Model enterprise value: **~RMB98.2bn**

On the model, conservative net cash represents approximately:

**RMB151.8bn / RMB250.0bn ≈ 61% of market capitalization**

The reported Q2 2026 balance sheet is even more liquid before conservative adjustments.

## Free Cash Flow

JD reported:

- **Q2 2026 FCF:** RMB31.8bn
- **TTM FCF to June 2026:** RMB31.4bn

My valuation framework uses approximately **RMB30bn of 2026 FCF**.

At a model market cap around RMB250bn:

**P / FCF ≈ 8.3x**

This is before giving explicit credit to the large net-cash position.

- Annual dividend: **US$1.00 per ADS**

### H1 2026

- Share repurchases: **US$1.0bn**
- Shares repurchased: **~2.5%** of 2025 year-end ordinary shares

At ~$26.50 per ADR, the $1 annual dividend corresponds to approximately **3.8%**.

## Founder Ownership

Richard Qiangdong Liu:

- founder and Chairman;
- approximately **12.5% economic ownership** in the latest founder filing;
- approximately **71.8% of total voting power**.

This creates meaningful alignment but also concentrated-control risk.

## Automation / AI Evidence

Q2 2026 examples include:

- thousands of autonomous delivery vehicles in regular operations across >20 provinces;
- 24-hour / night-time autonomous-delivery routes in Shenzhen;
- JoyInside partnerships with nearly 200 brands;
- connected JoyInside devices more than tripled versus the previous 11.11;
- AI-powered procurement tools in JD Industrials.

These are treated as **optionality**, not embedded aggressively into the base valuation.
