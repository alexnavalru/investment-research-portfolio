## Valuation Framework

The model emphasizes per-share cash generation rather than assuming a return to PayPal's historical premium multiples.

Illustrative 2026E values from the model:

- **10x FCF/share:** approximately **$73.6/share**
- **12x FCF/share:** approximately **$88.3/share**
- **14x FCF/share:** approximately **$103.1/share**

These are scenario outputs, not price targets.

## Capital Allocation

The model explicitly tracks:

- gross share repurchases;
- stock-based compensation dilution;
- net reduction in diluted shares;
- dividend payments;
- debt and liquidity;
- FCF after normal operating investment.

PayPal's Q2 2026 filing reported **$15.3bn of cash, cash equivalents and investments**, **$13.4bn of debt**, and approximately **$6.0bn of repurchases over the trailing twelve months**.

## Main Modeling Risk

A key analytical issue is the timing effect from Buy Now, Pay Later receivables. Because originations and subsequent sales can distort operating cash flow between periods, I normalize this effect when assessing sustainable FCF rather than relying mechanically on one reported period.
